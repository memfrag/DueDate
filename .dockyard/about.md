DueDate is a native macOS app for tracking subscriptions, recurring bills, renewals and
upcoming charges. It helps you see what you pay for, when each service renews, how it
renews, and where it can be managed or cancelled — before it costs you money.

Everything stays on your Mac: DueDate is local-first, with no account to create.

**Features:**

- Track streaming services, phone and internet plans, domains, hosting, and software licences
- Handles the different ways things renew — App Store, PayPal, card on file, Autogiro
- Reminders before a charge happens
- See what you're spending per month and per year
- Records where and how to cancel or manage each subscription
